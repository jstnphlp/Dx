# Reports and analytics

## Outcome

- Summarize project health, outcome pipeline, team capacity, and department workload from the shared operational prototype.
- Production reporting, exports, retention, and financial metrics are excluded pending business decisions.

## Access and data

- Every authenticated starter user can view the local prototype.
- Reports use derived browser state and do not query or mutate Supabase.

## Acceptance

- `/reports` shows live summary metrics and the four preview 6 report sections.
- Project, review, schedule, and work-session changes are reflected consistently.
- Content surfaces follow `DESIGN.md` and do not use repeated Liquid Glass cards.
